About VoteRwa

VoteRwa is Rwanda’s premier digital voting solution designed specifically for primary and secondary schools. We believe that the leaders of tomorrow should be elected using the technology of today.

Our Mission: To eliminate election fraud and paper waste in schools while teaching students the importance of a fair and transparent democratic process.

Why Choose Us?

Traditional voting is slow and often leads to disputes. Voterwa provides an unhackable system that gives instant results. Whether it's electing a Head Boy, Head Girl, or Class Prefects, our platform ensures every selected student's voice is heard accurately.
