How can we help?

I can't log in?

Ensure you are using the ID provided by your school (e.g., STD001). If it still fails, your teacher may not have registered you yet.

Can I vote twice?

No. Once you click "Submit," your ID is recorded, and you cannot access the ballot again.

Is my vote secret?

Yes. The teacher can see the total counts and winners, but they cannot see which specific student voted for which candidate.

The "Submit" button isn't working?

Make sure you have selected one candidate in every single category on the page.
